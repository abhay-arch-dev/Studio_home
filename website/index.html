Assumptions

  • Sizes come from the floor plan (room 3.66 × 3.745 m, pantry 1.73 × 1.80 m, balcony 2.65 × 0.91 m). Furniture sizes are estimated from the photos.
  • The TV moves to the right wall above the new TV unit. Re-use the same bracket, and fill the old holes with white wall putty; no repainting needed. The TV unit needs a plug point behind it for the TV and fridge. If there isn't one there, run a concealed extension from the pantry sockets.
  • The fridge niche has an open back and 5 cm of space around the fridge so it can breathe. Leave the back panel off.
  • The two single beds are joined into one double. Add a mattress joiner strip so the middle doesn't sag.
  • The wardrobe is freestanding: empty it, then move it to the far wall. Its mirror ends up facing the bed.
  • The split AC, its outdoor unit and the RO purifier are where they are in your photos. Leave about 20 cm between the outdoor unit and the washing machine.
  • The washing machine needs a socket, a water tap and a drain on the balcony's right side.
  • Finish swatches show how the furniture would look with a new laminate or wrap. Pick one for all furniture, or mix them per item. Coloured finishes are shown as matte laminates.
