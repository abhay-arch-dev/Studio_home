Assumptions

  • Sizes come from the floor plan (room 3.66 × 3.745 m, pantry 1.73 × 1.80 m, balcony 2.65 × 0.91 m). Furniture sizes are estimated from the photos.
  • The TV moves from the left wall to the toilet wall, facing the bed. Re-use the same bracket. The old screw holes on the left wall can be filled with white wall putty, no repainting needed. The black plastic chair from the photos is used at the desk.
  • The fridge and oven rack sits where the counter ends, near the counter plug points. Plug the oven and fridge into separate sockets, and the induction into its own 16A socket.
  • The split AC, its outdoor unit and the RO purifier are drawn where they are in your photos. The outdoor unit sits on brackets above the washing machine, so leave about 20 cm of air gap between them.
  • The washing machine needs a socket, a water tap and a drain point on the balcony's right side. If the tap or drain is on the left side, swap the machine with the plants.
  • The wardrobe is freestanding, so it only needs to slide about 50 cm toward the window. Empty it before moving it.
  • The two single beds are joined into one double, with a double comforter over both. Add a mattress joiner strip so the gap in the middle doesn't sag. You can still split them for two guests.
  • Finish swatches show how the furniture would look with a new laminate or wrap. Pick one for all furniture, or mix them per item. Coloured finishes are shown as matte laminates. The real furniture stays walnut unless you re-laminate it.
